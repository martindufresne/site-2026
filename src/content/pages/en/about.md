## Martin Dufresne - Teacher & R&D Researcher // Complex Systems & Software Morphogenesis

**Hello!** My name is Martin Dufresne. My work revolves around a central intuition: what if our software were no longer rigid structures, but organisms capable of growth? At the intersection of agentic AI, quantum computing, and cybersecurity, I develop models where form and function emerge from simple local rules.

## Skills and Expertise

To teach cybersecurity or quantum computing is to transmit the art of managing the unpredictable. I guide the next generation of specialists toward a critical vision of technology: no longer just using the tool, but understanding the laws of its genesis to better secure it.

## Innovation and Problem Solving

My current research focuses on Software Morphogenesis. Rather than building "Top-Down" systems, I design autonomous agents whose intelligence and topology evolve through neuroevolution. This approach allows the creation of cyber-resilient infrastructures that, like living organisms, heal after an attack and adapt organically to their environment.

## Global Perspective, Local Impact

Through my publications and research projects, I contribute to global thinking on the convergence of digital and biological systems. Locally, I am committed to **knowledge transmission**, training the next generation of specialists so they master cutting-edge technologies with a critical and enlightened vision.

## Lifelong Learning

The speed of evolution of AI and Quantum computing requires constant vigilance. This site reflects my moving thoughts and my commitment to remaining at the frontier of knowledge, transforming each new technological challenge into an opportunity for learning and sharing.

## Collaboration and Communication

I firmly believe that major breakthroughs are born from the porosity between disciplines. Whether popularizing complex concepts or collaborating on sharp research projects, I favor an open dialogue between experts, students, and science enthusiasts.

## Let's Collaborate

Are you working on a project at the crossroads of AI and biology? Would you like to exchange ideas on the future of cybersecurity or quantum technologies? **Let's work together to push the boundaries of form and function**.
